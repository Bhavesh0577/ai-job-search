## STAR Format

Structure answers as: **Situation** (context), **Task** (your responsibility), **Action** (what you did), **Result** (outcome).

Keep answers to 1-2 minutes. Be specific. End with what you learned or would do differently.

## Common Tough Questions

### "Why are you looking for a new role?"
> [Frame positively around growth, target domain, and new challenges.]

### "Tell me about a time you failed"
> [Situation + immediate ownership + corrective action + what you learned.]

### "Where do you see yourself in 3-5 years?"
> [Align with the role's growth path and your career goals.]

### "What is your biggest technical weakness?"
> [Real growth area, framed constructively, with steps already taken to improve.]

## Questions You Should Ask Interviewers
1. What does the day-to-day developer workflow look like here?
2. What are the biggest technical or architectural challenges the team is currently solving?
3. How are product decisions balanced between rapid feature delivery and scalability?
4. What does success look like for this role in the first 6 months?
